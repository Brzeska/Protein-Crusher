CURRENT PROJECT CITATIONS — TAU / MAP2 FIBRIL ENERGETICS
Compiled and checked: 2026-11-03

Scope: references for the structures, predictions, and proposed relaxation
workflow discussed so far. This is a working bibliography, not an exhaustive
literature review. Status distinguishes actual inputs from proposed methods;
listing a method does not mean it has been run or its parameters adopted.

1. Fitzpatrick, A. W. P., et al. (2017).
   Cryo-EM structures of tau filaments from Alzheimer's disease.
   Nature, 547, 185–190.
   https://doi.org/10.1038/nature23002

   Relevance: The experimental source of our AD tau reference structure,
   5O3L (paired helical filament), and its straight-filament counterpart,
   5O3T. Establishes the observed fold and protofilament packing against
   which we compare predictions and assess our preparation protocol.
   Status: Direct structural reference. The experimental fold does not
   guarantee that a truncated finite stack will remain stable under every
   simulation model.

2. Protein Data Bank entry 5O3L (released 2017).
   Paired helical filament in Alzheimer's disease brain.
   https://doi.org/10.2210/pdb5o3l/pdb
   https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5O3L

   Relevance: The coordinate dataset underlying the full-filament and
   single-protofilament reference files in this folder. Cite alongside
   Fitzpatrick et al. when identifying the structural input.
   Status: Direct input. See STRUCTURE_PROVENANCE.md for the local file
   history and chain selection. This is a dataset citation, not another paper.

3. Guo, L., Yu, Q., Wang, D., Wu, X., Wolynes, P. G., & Chen, M. (2025).
   Generating the polymorph landscapes of amyloid fibrils using AI: RibbonFold.
   Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(16), e2501321122.
   https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2501321122
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12037047/

   Relevance: Describes the predictor that generated our tau, MAP2, and
   scrambled-tau candidate stacks. It constrains predictions to parallel,
   in-register amyloid architecture and also examines shuffled sequences.
   Status: Direct prediction-method citation. Predicted structures and their
   frequencies are not automatically physical equilibrium populations, and
   scrambled predictions are not guaranteed to collapse during relaxation.

4. Zhou, Y., Li, J., Nordberg, A., & Ågren, H. (2021).
   Dissecting the Binding Profile of PET Tracers to Corticobasal Degeneration
   Tau Fibrils.
   ACS Chemical Neuroscience, 12(18), 3487–3496.
   https://doi.org/10.1021/acschemneuro.1c00536
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8447187/

   Relevance: Although the title emphasizes CBD, the methods also specify
   histidine states for an AD tau model based on 5O3T:
     H329: both ring nitrogens protonated; charge +1 (HIP).
     H330: delta nitrogen protonated; neutral (HID).
     H362: epsilon nitrogen protonated; neutral (HIE).
     H374: epsilon nitrogen protonated; neutral (HIE).
   Status: Informs a candidate starting assignment; not yet adopted in our
   files. These are computational preparation choices, not experimentally
   measured protonation states. Their 5O3T model differs from our 5O3L packing.

5. Eastman, P., et al. (2017).
   OpenMM 7: Rapid development of high performance algorithms for molecular
   dynamics.
   PLOS Computational Biology, 13(7), e1005659.
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005659

   Relevance: Foundational reference for OpenMM, the proposed software for
   evaluating forces and relaxing atom coordinates under a molecular model.
   Status: Proposed software. OpenMM is the calculation engine; force-field,
   solvent, protonation, and restraint choices still require specification.

6. Eastman, P., et al. (2024).
   OpenMM 8: Molecular Dynamics Simulation with Machine Learning Potentials.
   The Journal of Physical Chemistry B, 128(1), 109–116.
   https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.3c06662
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10846090/

   Relevance: Updated OpenMM software reference, potentially appropriate if
   we use version 8. Including it does not commit us to a machine-learning
   potential; the proposed workflow can use a conventional force field.
   Status: Proposed software citation; record the actual version when used.
